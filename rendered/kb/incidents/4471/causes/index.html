Engineering closed #4471 on 5 October. parser is fixed by fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. hint is won't fix, because the booking-v3 form is retired. These are two outcomes, so neither one speaks for the other.

The two problems

parser: filed as macyork/mega-what-it-shows#1. The shared parser reads every date month first, so a day-first tenant’s dates come out flipped. It reaches every tenant here, on the app, email and form channels.

hint: filed as macyork/mega-what-it-shows#2. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, so people type month first, and the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. It reaches only the day-first tenants on booking-v3, and only through the form channel. A change to the parser does not touch it. Nobody will fix the form, because it is retired. Its reports end when the form leaves service, so they do not prove the parser fix worked.

How to tell them apart

Use the tenant and the channel, not the subject or the intake tag. The rule is in src/lib/kb/causes.ts:

  1. Look up the tenant’s locale and form in the tenant directory.
  2. A report through the form channel, from a tenant on booking-v3, is hint.
  3. Any other report from a day-first tenant is parser.

The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser, so a reporter on an en-US browser gets hint wherever they work. The “tagged as” column shows where that goes wrong. The subject does not help either: the same subjects appear under both causes, which is how subject matching in decision 0001 put both into one ticket.

Not explained

The desk read the reports in the note under the table as a one-day move, not a flipped date. For example, one says the invite showed Tuesday 12 May and the calendar showed Monday 11 May. A month-first misreading does not explain that. They stay counted under the cause their tenant gives, and nobody has confirmed the cause. The parser fix may not cover them, so a new one-day report is not a regression of that fix.
